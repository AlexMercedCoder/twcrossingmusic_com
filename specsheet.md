# Terry W. Crossen – One-Page Website Specification  
**Artist Type:** Traditional Country Music  
**Primary Goal:** Grow and engage fan base  
**Target SEO Terms:** Florida Country Music, Florida Country Singer, Terry W. Crossen  

---

## 1. Visual Design & Color Scheme

**Overall Aesthetic:**  
Traditional, roots-oriented country with a Florida influence. The design should feel authentic, warm, and grounded. Avoid anything overly modern, glossy, or pop-styled.

**Primary Colors:**
- Dark Brown (#3A2A1F) – primary background and header color
- Cream / Off-White (#F4F1EC) – main background and text contrast
- Denim Blue (#2F4E6F) – secondary accents, links, and section dividers
- Burnt Orange (#C65A2E) – call-to-action buttons and highlights

**Typography:**
- Headings: Slab serif or classic serif (e.g., Playfair Display, Merriweather)
- Body Text: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Lato, Open Sans)

**Imagery Guidelines:**
- Live performance photos
- Acoustic guitar imagery
- Outdoor or sunset tones evoking Florida and traditional country roots

## 6. SEO Best Practices

**Meta Title:**  
Terry W. Crossen – Florida Country Singer-Songwriter

**Meta Description:**  
Discover Terry W. Crossen, an award-winning Florida country singer-songwriter delivering traditional country music with honest lyrics and classic sound.

**On-Page SEO:**
- One H1 using artist name and descriptor
- H2s for About, Music, Videos, and Connect
- Natural use of keywords: Florida Country Music, Country Singer-Songwriter
- Alt text for all images (e.g., “Terry W. Crossen performing live in Florida”)

**Technical SEO:**
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Fast load times and compressed images
- Clean anchor-based URLs
- Social profiles linking back to the official website
